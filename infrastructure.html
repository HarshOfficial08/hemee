Our Facility

World-Class Manufacturing Infrastructure

Our integrated facility at Nandasan, Mehsana is built for scale, precision and international compliance.

The Facility

Integrated Seed-to-Export Plant

Located on N.H. No. 8 in Nandasan, Mehsana — our campus houses two major production units operating in a fully integrated chain, from raw castor seed to finished export-grade product.

Seed Crushing & Solvent Extraction Plant

First stage in our integrated process — extracting castor oil from raw seeds at industrial scale.

High-capacity seed processing and expeller line for maximum oil recovery

Hexane solvent extraction system with closed-loop solvent recovery

De-solventizing and toasting unit for de-oiled cake production

Zero-discharge effluent management and waste heat recovery systems

Continuous process monitoring with automated quality checkpoints

Advanced Refining & Chemical Processing Plant

Second-stage processing converting crude oil into high-value refined products and derivatives.

Multi-stage refining: degumming, neutralisation, bleaching & deodorisation

Winterisation unit for cold-test stable refined castor oil

Hydrogenation reactor for Hydrogenated Castor Oil (HCO) production

12-HSA crystallisation and DCOFA dehydration specialty chemical lines

✓

Distillation columns for high-purity fatty acid fractionation
