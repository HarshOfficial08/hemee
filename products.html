Our castor oil division produces refined and specialty-grade oils that meet USA (USP), European (BP), and Chinese Pharmacopoeia standards. Suitable for pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, lubricants, and polymer industries.

Pale Pressed Grade (PPG) Castor Oil Premium light color oil for specialized industrial applications
Extra Pale Pressed Grade (EPPG) Castor Oil Ultra-light color oil for exacting specialty applications
Low Moisture Castor Oil (100 / 200 PPM) Specialized low moisture variant for sensitive chemical processes
Neutralised Castor Oil (NCO) Treated to reduce free fatty acids for specific applications
Cold Pressed / Deodorised Castor Oil Premium extraction without heat, optionally deodorised

Our advanced chemical processing plant is equipped with hydrogenation, crystallisation, and dehydration capabilities to produce a vast array of high-purity derivatives for global manufacturing sectors.

Dehydrated Castor Oil Fatty Acid (DCOFA) For paints, varnishes, and alkyd resin production
Hydrogenated Castor Oil (HCO) Flakes / Powder / Granules — for cosmetics, waxes, and PVC stabilisers
Castor Meal Acid (CMA) For bioherbicide and plant nutrition applications
Castor Oil Methyl Ester (CME) Biodiesel feedstock and specialty chemical intermediate
Turkey Red Oil (TRO) Sulfonated castor oil for textiles, leather, and dyeing industries
Undecylenic Acid For antifungal pharmaceuticals and specialty polymers
Heptaldehyde Fragrance and flavour compound; pharmaceutical intermediate

Our Linseed Oil portfolio caters to the coating, painting, and construction industries with precise boiling and refining standards. Ideal for alkyd resins, wood finishes, and industrial coatings.

Raw Linseed Oil Natural drying oil straight from extraction
Refined Linseed Oil Purified for lighter color and better consistency
Boiled Linseed Oil Treated with drying agents for faster curing times
Double Boiled Linseed Oil Advanced treatment for rapid drying paints and varnishes
Degummed Linseed Oil Gums and impurities removed for specialty uses
Soya Lecithin (Liquid / Paste) Natural phospholipid emulsifier for food and pharma

A key strength of our integrated facility is the utilisation of every by-product. Our de-oiled cakes serve the organic fertiliser and animal feed industries globally, offering high nitrogen content and OMRI-certified grades.

Castor Seed Meal (Castor Pomace) Natural, organic nitrogen fertilizer for soil enrichment
Castor De-Oiled Cake (Pellets / Powder) Solvent-extracted meal with minimal residual oil
High Protein Castor Meal – N8 Certified grades containing minimum 8% Nitrogen
High Protein Castor Meal – N10 Premium concentrated grade with minimum 10% Nitrogen
Rape Seed Meal High-protein animal feed supplement; rich in amino acids
