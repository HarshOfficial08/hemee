Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be the most trusted global partner in the chemical and agri-commodity supply chain through innovation and quality. We aspire to set the benchmark for castor derivatives manufacturing on the world stage.

Our Mission

To deliver consistently high-quality castor oil derivatives and industrial chemicals to global buyers, while building long-term partnerships grounded in transparency, reliability, and sustainable manufacturing practices.

Our Legacy

Founded with Purpose, Built for Scale

Hemee International Private Limited was founded in October 2020 with a clear vision: to build a world-class, integrated castor oil manufacturing and export enterprise rooted in Gujarat — the global capital of castor production.

In a short span, we have rapidly scaled to become a premier manufacturer and exporter, trusted by buyers across USA, China, Europe, and beyond. Our integrated facility in Nandasan, Mehsana enables us to control quality at every step — from raw seed to finished export-grade product.

Today, Hemee International stands as a testament to what determined leadership and uncompromising quality standards can achieve in a globally competitive industry.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every decision we make — from the factory floor to the boardroom.

Quality Assurance

Every product undergoes rigorous testing against international pharmacopoeia and industry standards before leaving our facility.

Global Compliance

We adhere strictly to USA (USP), European (BP), and Chinese Pharmacopoeia standards enabling seamless export to all major markets.

Sustainable Manufacturing

Our integrated process minimises waste. Solvent recovery, zero-discharge systems, and organic by-product utilisation are core to our operations.

Customer Partnership

We build relationships, not just transactions. Long-term partnerships with transparent communication and reliable supply are our foundation.

At Hemee International, we believe that world-class quality and customer trust are the foundations of enduring business success.
