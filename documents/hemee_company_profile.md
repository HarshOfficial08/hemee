## SECTION 1 — COMPANY OVERVIEW

### 1.1 About Hemee International Pvt. Ltd.

HEMEE INTERNATIONAL PVT. LTD. is a renowned company specialising in the manufacturing and distribution of high-quality Castor Oil & Derivatives. Incorporated in India with a strong global presence, Hemee serves markets across more than 60 countries worldwide. With a strong commitment to innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction, the company has established itself as a trusted and leading name in the castor oil and specialty chemicals industry.

The company is dedicated to providing exceptional products across a diverse range of industries including pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, paints & coatings, lubricants, textile, rubber, agricultural, and industrial sectors. Every product is manufactured under stringent quality control processes and delivered with a commitment to excellence.
